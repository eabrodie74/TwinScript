# Contributed recordings

Recordings of words TwinScript has no real recording of, made by speakers with TwinScript's recorder and published
under CC BY 4.0 with their consent. A recording plays in TwinScript only after another speaker of the language has
confirmed it (`"checked_by"`), and it's labelled as a contributed recording with the speaker's region.

`index.json`: file name → `{"scope": "taiwanese", "text": "銀行", "reading": "gîn-hâng", "unit": "word" | "character" |
"phrase" | "sentence", "region": "Tâi-pak", "speaker": "name, login or anonymous", "license": "CC BY 4.0",
"consent": true, "issue": 34, "checked_by": "…"}`. Files: `<scope>/<name>.m4a`, mono AAC, at most 30 seconds and
1 MB, the silence before and after trimmed.
